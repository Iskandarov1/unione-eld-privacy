Unione ELD Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how EldSolution ("we", "us") handles information in the Unione ELD mobile app (the "App"). The App is an electronic logging device (ELD) app for commercial truck drivers. It shows hours-of-service records kept for the driver's motor carrier under U.S. Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) rules, 49 CFR Part 395.

The App is provided to drivers through their motor carrier. Your carrier creates your account, and the hours-of-service records in it are records your carrier is legally required to keep.

Information the App collects

  • Account information: your username and password when you sign in, and the profile your carrier set up for you: name, email, phone number, driver's license number and state, carrier, home terminal and assigned vehicle and co-driver.
  • Hours-of-service records: duty status events (off duty, sleeper berth, driving, on duty), their times and durations, the locations and odometer and engine-hour readings recorded with them, daily log certifications and signatures, trailer and shipping document numbers, and vehicle inspection reports (DVIRs).
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, a device identifier, app version and language. These are sent when you sign in so your carrier can see which device holds your current session.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the App crashes, a crash report with the error, device model and operating system version is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service.
  • Information you enter for a roadside inspection: an officer's email address when you email your logs, and the comment you enter when you send an ELD output file to the FMCSA.

The App does not read your contacts, photos or files, and it does not show ads. The App itself does not use your phone's GPS; locations in your logs come from the ELD device or your carrier's records.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show your hours-of-service clocks, logs, violations and inspection reports.
  • To let you show or send your records to safety officials during a roadside inspection, as FMCSA rules require.
  • To find and fix crashes and keep the App working.

Who we share it with

  • Your motor carrier and the fleet-management service it uses: your hours-of-service records belong to the carrier's legally required records.
  • Safety officials: only when you choose to show, email or send your logs during an inspection, to the email address or FMCSA service you choose.
  • Service providers that run parts of the App for us, such as Google Firebase for crash reports. They may use the data only to provide that service.
  • When the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from an authority.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Security

All data the App sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore).

How long it is kept

Hours-of-service records and their supporting documents are kept by your carrier for at least six months, as 49 CFR 395.8 requires, and may be kept longer under your carrier's policy or other laws. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us or by contacting your carrier. Records your carrier must keep under federal law cannot be deleted before the legal retention period ends.
  • Signing out removes your session from the device.

Children

The App is for licensed commercial drivers and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
